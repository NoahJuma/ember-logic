# Physics Chapter 7: Potential energy and energy conservation

Source: the privately supplied Young and Freedman, *University Physics with Modern Physics in SI Units*, 15th edition, chapter 7 (printed pages 229–262). The attached book remains in the private library and is not part of the public repository. The exercise prompts are bilingual adaptations preserving the chapter's numbering, givens and subparts; worked explanations and diagrams were created for the platform.

The mixed problems 7.37–7.80 integrate the sections, including calculus, experimental data and the challenge problem; 7.81–7.84 are the DNA passage problems. The bank also includes Q7.1–Q7.23, the opening question and the chapter's bridging problem: 126 total entries. Each entry offers writing, a hint, a worked answer and video help independently. Figures are local, have Swedish and English text alternatives, and solution-only plots appear within the solution.

The quiz contains 60 numerical variants, 20 conceptual questions and five graph questions. Each of the five sections has questions at all four levels, with learning, topic, mixed and chapter-test modes. The energy-diagram lab graphs U(x) = ½kx² + bx, calculates F = −dU/dx, turning points and allowed positions, and asks for a prediction before revealing results. It explicitly uses an ideal one-dimensional conservative model with k > 0.

Exercises 7.74 and 7.79 require reading the source carefully: 7.74 is interpreted as a limit from a small positive starting position (exactly x = 0 is an unstable equilibrium), and 7.79's plotted points and answers are approximate visual readings. The graph-fit answer in 7.77 uses a least-squares trend, so its numbers are approximate. The book and its answer figures remain private.
