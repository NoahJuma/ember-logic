WELCOME TO EMBER

Learn it. See it. Try it.

Start with Gate playground. Flip the input switches and connect each rule to its truth table.

Build a circuit of up to 10 gates in Circuit workbench, choose connections, and trace every intermediate result. Next, use Boolean studio to solve expressions one operation at a time. Then visit Flip-flop lab: change an input, raise the clock, and watch what Q remembers.

Explore Number systems to compare binary, octal, decimal, and hex, inspect bytes, and practise exact conversions up to 64 bits. Explore Memory & storage: inspect cell construction, cache accesses, virtual pages, SSD/SD flash updates, and hard-drive reads. Use ASCII & text to inspect all 128 ASCII codes and compare ASCII with UTF-8. Finish with Training room: 16 guided experiments plus exam questions. Explore Signed binary for two’s complement, overflow, bitwise operations, and carries. In Media and data, paint pixels, hear PCM samples, decode frames, and reinterpret bytes. Each simulation has a live 3D inspector: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, choose Underside, or separate layers. Use Clear beside an input to enter a new value. Predict before you reveal. You get an explanation for every answer, and missed topics return more often.

These are ideal digital models. The memory lab uses rising-edge, synchronous flip-flops; real chips also have timing limits and may have asynchronous controls.
