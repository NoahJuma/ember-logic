    New to the notation? Read this first
    Variable and coefficient
    A variable is an unknown number. Its coefficient is the number multiplying it. In 3x, the variable is x and the coefficient is 3.
    Matrix, row and column
    A matrix is a grid of numbers. Rows go across and columns go down. A vector is an ordered list of coordinates, often written as a column.
    Augmented matrix
    Copy the coefficients of one equation into each row. The last column holds the numbers from the right of the equals sign.
    Pivot and free variable
    A pivot is a leading entry used to clear other entries in its column. A free variable has no pivot, so you can choose its value in a consistent system.
    Identity, inverse and transpose
    The identity I leaves a vector unchanged. The inverse A⁻¹ undoes A when it exists. The transpose Aᵀ swaps the rows and columns.
