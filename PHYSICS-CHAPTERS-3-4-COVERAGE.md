## Source-specific notes and verification

- In 4.18 the flea's mass is **215 µg**, as printed, not the corrupted PDF text extraction's mg.
- In 4.23 the printed wording says 100 N while its figure labels 250 N. The solution identifies the discrepancy and shows the results for both readings.
- In 3.8 the printed wording calls aₓ and aᵧ velocity components; the solution identifies them as acceleration components.
- The data graph in 3.76 is redrawn from approximate readings; results are accordingly estimates. In 3.82 the limiting speed depends on whether the camera must track the complete trajectory; the explanation states the model ambiguity.
- The automated suite checks complete numbering, bilingual structures, figures, the 132 generated numerical answer keys, representative source-specific pitfalls, state preservation and earlier platform behavior. Local browser checks covered chapter navigation, an exercise draft and solution across language change, mobile layout, and the projectile/force lab controls. Numerical and conceptual problems were also reviewed against the source and by calculation; this is not a proof that every transcription is error-free.
