It offers learning, topic, mixed-review and test modes. Written work and preferences stay in the current browser when storage is available. The lab models a uniform beam on two one-sided supports and linear elastic extension of a uniform wire; it warns when the two-support equilibrium would require a negative reaction.

Diagrams include some original English labels; bilingual descriptions and problem text explain them. In 11.18 and 11.45 the learner must estimate a personal force, so examples state that assumption. In 11.80 the problem and figure do not give the slab's weight, so the force is given in terms of its weight rather than inventing a number. In 11.82 the extension calculation includes the changing radius; 11.86 neglects the small extension in the pendulum geometry. Problem 11.85's fall calculation is a crude average-force textbook model, not a safety prediction. The elastic model does not validate real materials or engineering designs, and Young's modulus formulas apply only within the stated linear range.
