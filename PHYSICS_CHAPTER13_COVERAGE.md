The collection includes mixed, data and challenge problems 13.45–13.81, passage questions 13.82–13.84, Q13.1–Q13.21, the opening and bridging problems: 127 bilingual entries. Each offers writing, a hint and a worked explanation. The four-mode quiz contains 96 numerical variants and 32 conceptual items over eight sections and four levels. The lab explores ideal circular orbits and escape speed. Preferences and work are saved locally when browser storage is available.

Question text is adapted, not verbatim. Some diagram labels remain in English alongside bilingual explanations. 13.2 and 13.20 use stated estimates; 13.78 supplies the calculation method and order of magnitude, and the learner can use the source table for individual planet values. The orbit model neglects perturbations and assumes the radius is outside the central body. The black-hole examples use the textbook's simplified models.
