# Physics chapter 6: Work and kinetic energy

Source: the privately supplied 15th-edition Young and Freedman *University Physics with Modern Physics in SI Units*, chapter 6. The source PDF is not included in the repository.

The chapter includes four bilingual lessons (work; kinetic energy and the work–energy theorem; varying forces and springs; power); all 97 numbered exercises, 24 discussion questions, 12 guided variation problems, the bridging problem, the opening question, and four section checks. Original bilingual solutions use five teaching steps. Local vector figures and text alternatives accompany the figures; the quadratic fit for 6.91 appears only in its solution.

The 68-question quiz contains numerical and conceptual questions in four topics and four levels, including textbook-linked questions. It supports learning, focused, mixed and chapter-test modes. The independent work–energy lab visualizes a linear force against position, requests a prediction first and checks whether motion can reach the selected endpoint.

Source ambiguity: exercise 6.7 prints unequal block displacements although the blocks share an ideal taut string. Its explanation uses the given values for each separate work calculation and flags the incompatibility. VP6.2.1 makes explicit the condition on contact-point displacement that is required for the printed reciprocal-work answer.
